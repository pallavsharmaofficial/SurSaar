Last updated 7 October 2026

What this is

SurSaar is a free app for learning guitar. It listens through the microphone and, if you allow it, shows you the camera, and tells you what it heard: which chord, whether you were on the beat, which finger to move. It is a practice aid. It is not a replacement for a teacher, and it does not make anyone a musician by itself.

Its feedback is a guide

Chord and timing recognition is done by software listening to a guitar in a room, and it will sometimes be wrong: a noisy room, an out-of-tune string, a quiet strum or a phone placed far away can all make it name the wrong chord or miss a hit. When your ears and the app disagree, trust your ears, and use the tuner before you judge a chord. Scores and streaks are for motivation and mean nothing beyond that.

Songs and chord charts

The catalogue holds chords, key, capo, tempo and strumming for songs, marked as community-sourced and to be checked against the tutorial video. It does not contain lyrics. Chord charts can contain mistakes, and your voice or your guitar may want a different capo.

Songs you add by pasting a chord sheet stay on your device, and you are responsible for having the right to use what you paste. SurSaar does not publish it. A song request you submit on GitHub is public and is handled by an automated workflow that reads chord sheets from a short list of sites that allow it; lyrics are never stored.

Permissions and your surroundings

The microphone and camera are used only while you practise or tune, and you can refuse either. Play at a sensible volume, and do not look at the screen while moving about. Take breaks: practising for hours can hurt your hands.
