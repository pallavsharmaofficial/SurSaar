How it works

1

Pick a song, a lesson or just a few chords

Search the catalogue, follow a course from the first open chords to barre chords and Bollywood rhythms, or start an ad-hoc drill with any chords and strumming pattern.

2

Turn on the camera and mic

Hand tracking finds your fretting hand and paints each finger with its string and fret. The microphone feeds a chord recogniser and an onset detector. A metronome counts you in.

3

Play — and get corrected live

Wrong chord? It names what it heard. Late strum? It tells you to relax onto the click. Missed the change? It calls the next chord a beat ahead. Your score and history are saved locally.

What's inside

👀

Finger guidance in AR

MediaPipe hand landmarks map your fingertips to the target chord shape and flag straight or misplaced fingers.

🎧

Chord recognition

A chroma-based recogniser compares what you play to the target and to all 24 triads, so it can say "that sounds like Em".

🥁

Strumming & timing

Every strumming pattern becomes an eighth-note grid. Onsets are matched to slots: hit, early, late, missed.

🎼

Chord diagrams for any chord

49 stored open shapes plus movable E- and A-shape barres derived on the fly, with capo transposition.

📚

Courses or ad hoc

Guided paths with progress, or a two-tap quick practice. Lessons drill chords, strumming, technique or a whole song.

🔎

Song search & requests

Find songs by title, artist, chord or tag. Missing one? Request it and the ingestion pipeline adds chords, key, capo and strumming.

🔒

Private by design

Audio and video never leave the device. Progress lives in your browser or phone. No account needed.

🌐

Web + mobile, one codebase

Flutter app for the web (this site), Android and iOS. Hindi and English.

Songs in the catalogue

Bollywood classics to start with. Chords, key, capo and strumming come from the community and are marked as such — lyrics are never bundled.

Roadmap

  • Now — Web app with camera hand tracking, chord and timing coaching, courses, song search and requests. Mobile app with audio coaching and chord diagrams.
  • Next — Fretboard registration (knowing which fret a finger is on), hand tracking on Android/iOS, sampled metronome and backing loops, downloadable chord sheets.
  • Later — Tabs and single-note melodies, personalised drills from your mistakes, piano and vocals, cloud sync and sharing.
