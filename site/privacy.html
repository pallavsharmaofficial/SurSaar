Privacy

Camera and microphone stay on your device. The web app asks the browser for camera and microphone access only when you press play in a practice session. Video frames are analysed by MediaPipe hand tracking running in your browser; audio is analysed by a chord and onset detector written in Dart, also in your browser. No audio, video or landmarks are ever sent to a server.

Your progress is local. Sessions, favourites, lesson progress and settings are stored in your browser's local storage (or the app's private storage on a phone). Clearing site data removes them. There are no accounts.

Content is fetched from GitHub. The song and lesson catalogue is a public JSON file in the project repository. Loading it makes a normal HTTPS request to GitHub; the app also works fully offline from a bundled copy. The hand-tracking model and its runtime are fetched from jsDelivr and Google's model storage the first time the camera is used.

No analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
