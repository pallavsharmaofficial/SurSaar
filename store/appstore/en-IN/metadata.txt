REVIEW NOTES
The app has no account and no server of its own, so there is nothing to sign in
to. On launch it downloads one public JSON file of songs from GitHub and falls
back to the copy bundled inside the app when offline. The microphone is used
only during a practice session or in the tuner, to analyse the chord or pitch
on the device; nothing is recorded or uploaded. The camera is used only during a
practice session, as a live preview of the user's own hands; frames are not
analysed, saved or sent. The photo library is used only when the user taps
Change Photo on the Profile tab. Every screen works with all three refused.
To try it: open Home, tap Tuner and pluck a string, or open Learn, choose a song
and start a practice session. The app is free with no purchases.
